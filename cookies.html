Cookie Notice

Cookies and similar technologies

This notice explains what browser storage and similar technologies are used on the Melody website.

Last updated: June 3, 2026

Melody does not set analytics, advertising, profiling, or marketing cookies on this website. Because no non-essential cookies are set by Melody, the site does not display a consent banner.

Strictly necessary processing

Hosting providers may process basic technical information, such as IP address, requested page, browser details, and timestamps, to deliver the site, maintain security, and diagnose service issues. This is necessary for the website to function.

Contact form

The contact form submits information to Formspree only when a visitor chooses to send a message. Formspree may use technical measures needed to deliver and protect the form service.

Employee login

Employee access uses Google sign-in for approved team accounts. Google may set cookies or use similar technologies as part of its authentication service.

Analytics and marketing

Melody does not load analytics, advertising, or marketing tracking on this website. Any non-essential tracking introduced by Melody should be presented with clear information and consent controls before it is used for UK and EU visitors.
